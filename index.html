Keep everyone safe — it's easier than you think

Snakes don't want trouble. Nearly all bites happen when someone tries to catch or kill a snake — so don't. Do this instead:

1 · Keep your distance

Move people well back. From a few metres away, a snake is no threat to you.

2 · Watch where it goes

If it's safe, keep eyes on the snake from a distance. Knowing where it is makes the catch quick.

3 · Secure pets & kids

Bring dogs, cats and children inside or well away from the area.

4 · Call Kev

Ring 0422 267 151 any time, day or night. You'll get straight-up advice immediately.
